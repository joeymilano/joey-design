# Art direction in use

Start with the material. For Fold Frequency it is the cut edge of a blue aluminium fold. In a deck that edge leads the eye across a page. In the note the reader inspects it in two crops. In the instrument the eye follows a waveform while the ear compares timbres.

## Print three proofs

Run the directions command on a deck brief. Open the generated index.html to compare the three directions and switch language or orientation. The command writes 12 JPGs covering three compositions, two languages and landscape/portrait. A main photograph with breathing room and a distinct detail image produce the widest range. Use the folded sculpture originals to reproduce the shipped study.

| Proof | Where the eye lands | When to choose it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field | Large title on open space beside the object's silhouette | The object and background stay legible together at 240 px |
| Catalogue | Ink on uncoated paper, an inset image and an uninterrupted baseline | The viewer needs to inspect an object or compare precise details |
| Detail | Material texture fills the page, title sits on an opaque lower strip | The surface carries the story, or a busy photograph gives type nowhere to sit |

Put proofs side by side at the same width. Cover up their titles. If two designs become difficult to tell apart, change the proportions or move from the whole object to a specific detail. Check the body and title at phone-feed scale. Try a title twice as long and revise line breaks independently in each language. Recompose the portrait as a new page with room for a thumb and caption.

## Carry the choice through

- Project the slide with the largest title and the slide with the most evidence. Keep the opening image large. Follow it with a decisive change of scale, a numerical or photographic comparison, and an action someone in the audience can take. Review the native PowerPoint file.
- Open the social pack at feed size. Look at the first, middle and final cards together: introduction, observation, action. Read the instruction on one card and check whether its image actually demonstrates it.
- Treat the App as the part a person gets to manipulate. A sound study earns a working instrument. In an instrument test, change only the waveform at a fixed pitch, then only pitch at a fixed waveform. Save, reload, recover the patch and export the recording.
