# Art direction in practice

| Direction | Choose it for | Composition and type | Image treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nocturne | An object launch, prototype review or demo-day reveal | A dark split cover. Large sans-serif title at left. A warm rule picks out the product lighting. Switch to a light ground for the specification page. | Isolate the silhouette against a continuous background. Photograph the mechanism at working angle. Keep the hinge, controls and contact shadow visible. |
| Folio | Cultural programmes, independent publishing or a craft story | An inset plate on a paper field. Set the title in a book serif and the caption in a measured utility face. Give the object room to breathe. | Show the physical marks that establish a medium, such as paper grain, ink or tool marks. Supply a dedicated portrait crop for a detail. |
| Signal | A study, technical field report or operational briefing | A horizontal evidence strip opens the deck. The headline and interpretation occupy different columns. Use aligned measures on the proof page. | Choose a plot, diagnostic image or measured specimen. Label units and provenance. Supply a wide composition that can be read from the back row. |

## Across formats

- **Talk** — use the slide title as the spoken claim. Keep methodological qualifications in visible copy when they change how a number should be read. Put the transition in speaker notes.
- **Sales page** — place the object and the reason to care above the fold. Write a complete action such as “Request a dealer sample”. Point it at a supplied destination. A concept may link to its source notes.
- **Portrait cover** — read at 180 px wide. Give the silhouette and headline separate space. Remove detail that cannot survive a thumbnail.
- **English / Chinese** — retain approved product names. Translate benefits idiomatically. Check local punctuation and prohibited line starts. Avoid automatically rounding or converting a specification during translation.

## Critique the evidence

Read the headline with the image covered. Then cover the headline and read the image. Each should add information. Trace one fact from the source to the deck, website and notes. Test the actual decision link. A layout check can find a line that does not fit. A buyer or audience member must still judge whether the story is convincing.
